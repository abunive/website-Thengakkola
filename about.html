ABOUT

Establishment & Academic Focus

The Department of Information Technology was founded in the year 2000 as part of GEC Idukki’s growth of academic offerings . The B.Tech in Information Technology program equips students with a robust foundation in computing—covering programming languages, networking, data management, software design, and system administration

Infrastructure & Facilities

The department boasts modern infrastructure: high-speed internet access, smart classrooms outfitted with LCD projectors and digital podiums, and advanced computing labs with up-to-date development tools .

Faculty & Development Initiatives

The department is supported by well-qualified, dedicated faculty members. They actively encourage student participation in extracurricular and co-curricular activities, and also conduct short-term training programs to keep both faculty and students updated with the latest technological trends .

Career Outcomes & Training

IT grads from GEC Idukki are prepared for a wide range of roles—programming, web/mobile development, networking, cybersecurity, database administration, systems architecture, IT management, and more .

Reviews from platforms like Shiksha highlight strong placement support. Many students receive offers in the 3–6 LPA range, with companies such as TCS, Wipro, Experian, IBM, Google, Infosys, and others actively recruiting. Internship opportunities and placement guidance are also available .

Student Experience & Reputation

Students often praise the department and college for its peaceful hill-station setting, supportive infrastructure, and quality faculty—a perfect environment for focused learning .

The college overall enjoys a solid reputation: it’s government-run, AICTE-approved, KTU-affiliated, and well-known for its active student clubs and events
