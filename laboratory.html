LABORATORY

FACILITIES

Software Lab

Students are trained with programming languages, databases, and tools. Equipped with Turbo C, Python, JDK, Oracle, Dreamweaver, Netbeans, Network Animator. 32 students can work simultaneously.

Hardware Lab

Students do data structures and hardware experiments. 32 students can work at once with individual laptops.

Machine Learning Lab

Students work on network programming and microprogramming. Contains 32 systems with multimedia facilities and software like Keil IDE, Packet Tracer, plus 28 microprocessor kits.
