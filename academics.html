ACADEMICS

PROGRAMS

B.TECH: The four year degree program has an intake of 69 including 6 lateral entry seats.

M.TECH: The department runs an M.Tech program with specialization in Network Engineering. The annual intake is 18.

CURRICULUM

  • Duration – 4 years (8 semesters).
  • Each semester has around 5 theory subjects + labs.
  • Credits system, minimum total required to pass.
  • Evaluation: Internal (50%) + University exam (50%).
  • Semester-wise:
    • Sem 1 & 2: Basics – Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Graphics, C, Electronics, Life Skills.
    • Sem 3 & 4: Core IT – Data Structures, DBMS, SE, COA, OS, Algorithms, Networks.
    • Sem 5 & 6: Advanced IT – ML, Cloud, IoT, Cybersecurity, Web Tech, Electives.
    • Sem 7 & 8: Specialization & Projects – DL, Blockchain, Research, Internship, Final Year Project.
  • 🔹 Extra Parts of Curriculum
    • Mini Project – In middle semesters.
    • Internship – Compulsory training in industry.
    • Seminar – Presentation & communication skills.
    • Main Project – In final year.
    • Activity Points – From co-curricular / social activities
