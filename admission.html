ADMISSION

HOW TO?

Admission is through entrance exams and counselling. For B.Tech: KEAM exam (50% KEAM + 50% Class 12 marks). For M.Tech: GATE + counselling.

B.Tech eligibility: 10+2 with PCM (45–50%). M.Tech eligibility: B.E./B.Tech with 50% marks.

Fees: ₹20,000–34,000 per year (B.Tech), ₹26,000 (M.Tech).

HOSTEL DETAILS

• Ladies' Hostels at GEC Idukki

Number & Location: There are two government-run ladies' hostels, named Nila and Kabani, both located within the college campus

Capacity: Combined, the ladies' hostels can accommodate approximately 300 students . Administrative Setup: A warden oversees the hostels, supported by resident tutors. Specifically, Dr. Anwar T M (Mathematics & Basic Sciences Dept.) is the warden for the ladies' hostels

Fees: Rent: Around ₹310 per month . Hostel Development Fund (HDF): Approximately ₹750 per semester . Food/Mess: The mess bill is separate and varies based on attendance; typically ₹2,500–₹3,000 per month . Student reviews mention that the food quality is average, with offerings including chicken, egg, and fish twice a week

• Gents' Hostels at GEC Idukki

Availability & Location: Males have hostels named Men’s Hostel I, II, III, IV—some inside campus, others slightly away (e.g., near SBT or in Painavu/Cheruthoni) . Additionally, there are off-campus/private hostel options in Cheruthoni (~6 km away), including Wisdom Homes Boys Hostel and other privately run facilities .

Accommodation & Transport: GEC provides college bus service to these off-campus hostels .

Fees: Specific fee details for gents’ hostels aren't well-documented; however, general estimations from other sources suggest hostel services (including rent and food) cost around ₹65,100 (possibly annual or comprehensive) . User feedback elsewhere suggests rent is very low (around ₹300 per month), while the mess is ₹2,500–₹3,000 monthly. If the mess isn't consistently functional, relying on outside eateries may cost ₹4,000+ per month .
